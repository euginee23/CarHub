## 1. The parties

This agreement is between the **vehicle owner** and the **renter** named on the booking. CarHub is the marketplace that connects them. It is not a party to the rental and does not own, lease, or operate the vehicle.

## 2. Eligibility

The renter confirms that they:

- are at least 21 years of age;
- hold a driver's licence that stays valid for the whole rental period; and
- have submitted two valid, independently issued government IDs, both approved by CarHub before the rental proceeds.

## 3. Schedule

- The rental runs from the pickup date and time to the return date and time shown on the booking and contract.
- Rentals are charged for every started 24-hour period.
- The vehicle must be returned at the agreed time and place. Late returns are charged pro rata and may incur penalties where they disrupt a following booking.
- An active rental may be extended only when no other booking follows it, at the original daily rate.

## 4. Payment

- The total shown at checkout is the daily rate multiplied by the number of rental days, plus the CarHub service fee.
- Payment must be completed online through CarHub. A booking is only confirmed once payment has been validated.
- Payments made outside CarHub are not protected and are not permitted.

## 5. Cancellation

- The renter may cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before pickup.
- Cancellations within 24 hours of pickup forfeit the first day of the rental.
- If the owner cancels a confirmed booking, the renter receives a full refund.

## 6. Renter responsibilities

- Only the verified renter named on the booking may drive the vehicle.
- Operate the vehicle lawfully, never under the influence of alcohol or prohibited substances.
- Do not sublet, re-rent, or use the vehicle for commercial passenger transport.
- Return the vehicle with the same fuel level it was collected with.
- Report any accident, breakdown, or traffic violation to the owner and to CarHub without delay.
- Traffic violations, tolls, and parking penalties incurred during the rental are the renter's responsibility.

## 7. Owner responsibilities

- Provide a roadworthy, clean, and mechanically sound vehicle matching its listing.
- Hold valid registration and insurance for the vehicle.
- Honour the confirmed booking and hand the vehicle over at the agreed time and place.

## 8. GPS tracking

The renter consents to the vehicle's GPS unit reporting its location to the owner and to CarHub for the duration of the rental only. Tracking stops when the rental is closed, and trip location history is kept for 30 days for dispute resolution.

## 9. Damage and liability

Damage is assessed against the vehicle's condition recorded at pickup and at return. The renter is liable for damage caused during the rental beyond fair wear and tear, up to the limits set out in the CarHub terms and conditions.
